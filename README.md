# DroneProject

## What is this project?

Well, we were basically tasked to, in pairs, prototype and develop a sufficiently complex embedded system to show all that we had learned so far during our program. Me and my groupmate [@SamCodes200](https://github.com/SamCodes200) decided upon the simple task of achieving artificial flight in the form of our own drone, built from zero. While we did encounter a lot of hurdles along the way, we did technically achieve our goal of reaching a net positive thrust to weight ratio. This meant the drone did in fact lift off from the ground.

### What is in this repo?

The project wasn't executed in the most organized fashion, so this is primarily a publication of our results for later reference to myself or other interested parties. I humbly ask forgiveness for the unorganized files, they bother me too.
